Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Is DIMASCLIP free?

Yes. We're building around free and open-source tools, with no subscription planned for the core experience.

Can I create YouTube Shorts?

That's the main purpose of DIMASCLIP. Video cutting, vertical formatting and exporting are part of our development plan.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

The link field is currently a visual starting point. Direct importing will require a supported integration. You can use video files you own or have permission to edit.

Does every AI feature work already?

Not yet. The homepage is ready as a front-end. The video-processing and AI engines will be connected and tested separately.
